# Knowledge base and publication scope

[Home](../README.en.md) · [中文](knowledge-base.md)

The repository's `wiki/` organizes writing, filming, and competitive-research knowledge by topic and product category. Modules under `rag_system/retrieval/`, `embedding/`, and `storage/` support retrieval. Personal inputs and vector data normally stay local.

At the time of this review, local Wiki changes include rewrites, additions, and deletions. They have not been synchronized in bulk because their sources, quotations, and rights to publish require individual review. Existing historical repository content has not automatically been cleared for reuse by this review.

Record sources and check publication rights before adding material. Exclude client briefs, personal data, authentication files, and complete third-party works without permission. Review generated claims before use.
